How we work

An operating group, using its own capital

Oyaji Conglomerate LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L24000051367, filed in 2024, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We build and operate our own businesses with our own money. We are not a fund, a broker-dealer or an investment adviser, we are not registered with the SEC or the Florida Office of Financial Regulation, and we accept no capital from the public. Nothing here is an offer, a solicitation or a business-opportunity offering, and each operating business holds its own licences for the sector it trades in.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can I invest in the group?

No. We use our own capital only and accept no funds from anyone. There is no offering, and nothing on this site is a solicitation.

Do you invest in other people's businesses?

No. We build and operate our own. If you are looking for investment, we are not the right door and we would rather say that immediately.

Is this a franchise?

No. We do not sell franchises, licences or business opportunities, and nothing here is a franchise offering.

What sectors are you in?

Several, deliberately small in number at any one time. Each business holds its own licences and trades under its own entity.

Why close a business that is making money?

Because slightly profitable businesses consume management attention that a better one needs. Deciding that on purpose, annually, is the discipline.

How do I verify the company?

Search document number L24000051367 on the Florida Division of Corporations record at sunbiz.org.

Working on something adjacent?

A first conversation costs nothing. If we are not the right fit, we will say so quickly.
